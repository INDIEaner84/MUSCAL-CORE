## Architecture Findings

1. **resolve_evidence_status precedence was wrong**: `INFERRED` was checked before `VERIFIED`, causing `[INFERRED, VERIFIED]` to evaluate to `INFERRED` instead of `VERIFIED`. Fixed to CONFLICT > VERIFIED > INFERRED > MISSING.

2. **OutcomeRecord._resolve_status conflict blindness**: When observed and verified outcomes disagreed, evidence_status was set to the last-applied source instead of CONFLICT. Fixed to detect `observed != verified` and emit CONFLICT.

3. **OutcomeRecord binding validation**: The `MetaEvaluator.evaluate()` method accepted any OutcomeRecord without verifying its execution_id matches the requested execution. Added `_validate_outcome_record_binding()` guard.

4. **MetaEvaluation.agent_id was populated from trace_id**: `_evaluate_report` fell back to `exec_rec.trace_id` for agent_id. Fixed to read `exec_rec.agent_id` directly.

5. **ExecutionRecord was missing agent_id/model_id**: These fields existed on the receipt but were not captured in the resolved ExecutionRecord model. Added them to the dataclass and the resolver.

## Residual Risks

1. **MREIL metric source trust**: MREIL metrics are provided as a parameter to evaluate(). They are not independently verified. Their evidence_status in claims is VERIFIED based on the assumption the caller provided honest metrics.
2. **OutcomeRecord trust boundary**: The caller provides OutcomeRecord to evaluate(). The binding guard prevents cross-execution substitution but does not verify the outcome record's integrity hash.
3. **Validation artifact storage**: EvaluationValidation artifacts are returned in the MetaEvaluation but are not persisted to any store. They exist only in memory during the evaluation lifecycle.

## E3.6 Recommendation

E3.6 should address:
- **Persistent validation artifact store**: Write EvaluationValidation to a validation_artifacts table in the decisions DB, keyed by evaluation_id.
- **OutcomeRecord integrity verification**: When an OutcomeRecord is provided to evaluate(), verify its integrity_hash against its serialized content.
- **Cross-evaluation trend analysis**: Aggregate MetaEvaluation records for trend detection (e.g., degrading execution quality over retries).
- **Trust boundary audit trail**: Track the provenance of MREIL metrics and OutcomeRecord sources in the evaluation rationale.
